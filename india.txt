India has one of the world's oldest continuous civilizations.
The Indus Valley Civilization flourished around 2500 BCE.
Ancient India made major contributions to mathematics, astronomy, medicine, and philosophy.
The Maurya Empire was one of the earliest large empires in India.
Emperor Ashoka spread Buddhism across India and beyond.
The Gupta period is often associated with major developments in science, mathematics, art, and literature.
India later saw the rise of many regional kingdoms and powerful dynasties.
The Mughal Empire played an important role in shaping India's architecture, culture, and administration.
European trading companies arrived in India from the 16th century onward.
The British gradually established political control over large parts of India.
India's independence movement grew strongly during the 19th and 20th centuries.
India became independent from British rule on 15 August 1947.
